title: Riemann rectangles

**The idea:** the integral, before the limit — sample a curve, stand a
rectangle under each sample, and the area assembles bar by bar. The
`Scene.stagger` is doing the teaching here: watching the sum build is what
makes the picture mean something. Each bar grows from the baseline by
driving `height` and center-`y` together.
