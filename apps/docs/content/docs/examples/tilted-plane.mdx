---
title: Tilted plane
description: Blur that follows depth continuously across a floor receding to the horizon
---

**The idea:** depth of field is worked out per pixel, not per shape. The
floor here is a checkerboard of flat tiles tipped back a quarter turn, so a
single tile spans a whole range of depths — and the blur follows that depth
smoothly, with no steps between tiles. One band across the floor is sharp;
in front of it and behind it the tiles soften gradually.

The scene sweeps that band down the floor and back with a `focusDistance`
tween, then pushes in with `Camera.dollyTo` while tweening `focusDistance`
to the same distance with the same easing — a focus pull that keeps the
subject sharp as the camera moves.
