---
title: Depth orbit
description: Flat shapes at four depths become a volume the moment the camera orbits
---

**The idea:** nothing here has any thickness — four rings of flat squares
at four different depths around a core. It looks like a flat pattern until
the camera orbits, and then parallax separates the layers and the whole
arrangement snaps into being a *place*. That's the 2.5D premise in one
shot: depth is a property of `position.z`, and the camera does the rest.
