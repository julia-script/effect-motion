---
title: Camera follows the graph
description: Ride a curve wider than the frame, then pull back to reveal the journey
---

**The idea:** a graph too wide for the frame becomes a *journey* — the
camera rides along with a dot travelling the curve (`Camera.follow` copies
its position into the point of interest every frame), and only the
pull-back at the end reveals how far it went. Follow, then reveal: the
most cinematic move a plot can make, and it's two camera calls.
