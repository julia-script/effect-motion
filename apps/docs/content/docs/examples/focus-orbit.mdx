---
title: Focus orbit
description: A shallow lens swings around a sharp subject while the scatter around it melts
---

**The idea:** a wide-open lens orbiting a subject. Flat shapes are scattered
through depth around a sun at the origin; with a large `aperture`, anything
off the focus plane melts into soft shapes, and the sun stays crisp.

The camera starts at its resting distance, which is where `focusDistance`
defaults to, so the subject is sharp without setting anything. `Camera.orbitTo`
swings around the point of interest at a fixed distance, so the focus holds on
its own. `Camera.dollyTo` changes that distance, so the push-in tweens
`focusDistance` alongside it with the same duration and easing.
