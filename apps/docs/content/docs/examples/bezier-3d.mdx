---
title: Bézier in 3D
description: A cubic Bézier constructed in space — control points, control polygon, and a turntable camera
---

**The idea:** a cubic Bézier curve is a weighted blend of four control
points — and in 3D you can *see* the blend, because every sampled point
carries its own depth. The control polygon shows the scaffolding, labels
name the points, a wireframe box frames the volume, and the camera
turntables around it with `Camera.orbitTo` while `Camera.lookAt` pins the
aim — no orientation math anywhere in the scene.

<Example name="bezier-3d" />

The curve itself is the sampling trick again: 48 straight spans, each
endpoint at its own z, projected point by point.
