---
title: Streamlines
description: A vector field made visible — ninety advected lines, deterministic by construction
---

**The idea:** you can't draw a vector field, but you can release tracers
into it — ninety short `Line`s, each stepped along the field every frame by
a pure integrator in `Motion.drive`, tails trailing their local velocity.
The seed positions come from the scene's seeded `Random`, so this
"turbulent" flow replays *identically* on every run and every machine.
For a motion library, that's the punchline: determinism isn't the obstacle
to organic motion, it's what makes it shippable.
