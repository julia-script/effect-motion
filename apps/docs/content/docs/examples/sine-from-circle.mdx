---
title: Sine from a circle
description: The sine wave is circular motion, unrolled through time
---

**The idea:** the sine wave *is* circular motion, unrolled — a point moving
around a circle, with its height carried rightward through time, draws the
wave. One parameter `t` drives all four entities (the orbiting dot, the
height line, the tracer, and the growing wave) through `Motion.drive`, and
the frame barrier keeps them in exact lockstep.

<Example name="sine-from-circle" />

Worth noticing: the wave is a `Path` whose command list is *rebuilt from
`t` every frame* — growing a sampled curve is the whole "draw-on" trick,
and it's the same one the [function plot](/docs/examples/function-plot)
uses.
