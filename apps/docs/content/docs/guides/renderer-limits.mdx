---
title: What the renderer draws
description: Which entity fields reach the screen, how overlaps resolve, and what is not supported yet
---

Every entity field is part of the frame data, but not every field has a
visual effect. This page lists what `@effect-motion/renderer` draws today,
in the browser (studio, `Player`) and in Node (`motion frames`,
`motion render`). Both renderers draw the same things.

## Fields that render

Every paintable entity renders `position`, `rotation`, `scale`, `opacity`
and `visible`. Rotation and scale act about `position`, and all of them
compose down the tree: moving, fading, scaling or rotating a `Group`
carries its whole subtree.

| Entity | Its own fields |
|---|---|
| `Circle` | `radius`, `fillColor`, `strokeColor`, `strokeWidth` |
| `Ellipse` | `radiusX`, `radiusY`, `fillColor`, `strokeColor`, `strokeWidth` |
| `Rect` | `width`, `height`, `fillColor`, `strokeColor`, `strokeWidth` |
| `Line` | `start`, `end`, `strokeColor`, `strokeWidth` |
| `Path` | `commands` (`M`, `L`, `Z`), `fillColor`, `strokeColor`, `strokeWidth` |
| `Text` | `text`, `fontSize`, `fontFamily`, `fillColor`, `textAnchor`, `baseline` |
| `Image` | `image`, `width`, `height` (both, or neither for natural size) |
| `Group` | `children` (paints nothing itself) |
| `Hud` | `children`, drawn in screen space on top of everything |
| `Camera` | `position`, `rotation`, `poi`, `focalLength` |

A few details:

- **Facing.** A planar shape (`Circle`, `Ellipse`, `Rect`, `Text`, `Image`)
  with rotation 0 faces the camera. Any other rotation, its own or an
  ancestor's, makes it a real plane in 3D. `Line` and `Path` are always
  drawn in 3D space as authored.
- **Strokes** scale with the shape: the drawn width is `strokeWidth` times
  the narrower of `|scale.x|` and `|scale.y|` (ancestors included), so a
  shape scaled to zero on either axis draws nothing.
- **Group opacity** multiplies into each child. Children that overlap each
  other show through one another while the group fades. To fade a section
  as one flat layer, build it as its own scene and mount it with
  `Scene.play`, then fade the mount.
- **Camera `aperture` and `focusDistance`** are carried on the frame but
  have no visual effect: depth of field is currently disabled, and every
  frame renders sharp.

## Overlaps

Occlusion follows depth, not the order you created things in:

- Content nearer the camera (larger `z` under the default camera) hides
  content behind it, whatever the tree order.
- Content at the **same depth** paints in tree order, later over earlier,
  like layers in a compositing tool. Text on a card at the same `z` is
  drawn on top as long as the text is created after the card.
- Translucent content (`opacity` below 1) does not hide what is drawn after
  it: a fading card tints the text behind it instead of cutting holes.

## Images

In the browser an `Image` decodes anything the browser's
`createImageBitmap` supports. In Node (`motion frames`, `motion render`)
only **PNG and JPEG** decode; any other format fails loudly with the
image's id. Use PNG or JPEG for assets that must render in both. SVG is not
an image format here — trace it into `Path`s instead.

## Paths

`Path` commands are straight segments only: `M` (move), `L` (line) and `Z`
(close). There are no curves or arcs; sample a curve into points yourself.
Each closed subpath fills on its own, so there are no holes: a ring or the
counter of a letter needs separate shapes layered on top.

## Not available yet

Plan around these; none of them render today:

- masks, track mattes and wipes;
- per-letter text (a `Text` is one block; use one `Text` per word or line);
- blur of any kind, including motion blur and depth of field;
- lit or shaded 3D meshes;
- curves, arcs and holes in `Path`.
